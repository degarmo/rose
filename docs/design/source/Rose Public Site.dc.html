Who we are

Rose is a volunteer-run nonprofit club with a members' venue on Chicago's north side. We host a class every Thursday, social evenings on Fridays and Saturdays, and workshops and community events through the month.

We are inclusive, consent-first and non-judgmental. Nobody here will assume what you know or what you want. First-timers are welcome at every night we run.

Thursday class: communication

When
Thu, Oct 22 · 7:00–9:00 PM
Where
The classroom, 2nd floor
Cost
$15 · free for members

Clear asks, clear answers. This class covers how to say what you want, how to hear a no without taking it personally, and how to check in during an evening. We practice in pairs with scripts, then without.

Taught by two volunteer hosts. Come alone or with a friend; nobody is paired with someone they don't choose. Doors open at 6:30 for tea.
